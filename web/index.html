You choose the precision. The data returns the coverage.

Automating ticket routing is not one number. Demand a higher routing accuracy and fewer tickets qualify for automation. This instrument makes that trade visible, certifies a confidence threshold that holds on tickets nobody has seen, and refuses when the data cannot support what you asked for.

What the stamp means

The threshold was selected by risk control on a calibration sample the model never trained on, with the family-wise error across the whole threshold grid held at 10%.

Why it said that

Every decision carries the archive documents that produced it. A category that gets disputed can be inspected instead of argued about, which is the difference between analytics a manager is asked to trust and analytics a manager can audit.

What this is not

  • The encoder here is TF-IDF with truncated SVD, not the multilingual sentence model that belongs in production. Swapping it moves every accuracy number on this page. It does not change whether the threshold selection is valid, which is the thing being tested.
  • The corpus is Reuters newswire: well written, consistently labelled, monolingual. Support tickets are none of those. A published comparable project on Russian-language IT support reached 84% precision at 61% coverage, against human operators at 75%.
  • Two findings in an earlier draft of this work were artifacts of bugs the test suite later caught, including one that had already been recommended for a client proposal. The report says which.
